How to play

  • Tap one of your pieces, then a glowing cell. Shards are many-coloured crystal clusters; blobs are living jelly.
  • Green, next door: your piece splits, and a copy grows there.
  • Violet, two away: it leaps there and leaves its old cell empty.
  • Every enemy piece touching the cell you land on turns into one of yours.
  • If a side can't move, the game ends and the other side takes every empty cell. Most cells wins.
  • Drag to turn and tilt the board, pinch to zoom. The ⊕ button straightens it again.
  • Captures come with a random flourish: lightning, a shockwave, shattering, a goo splash or crystals bursting through.
